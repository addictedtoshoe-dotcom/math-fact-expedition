Questions from the crew

Before you board.

What ages and grades is this for? +
Math Fact Expedition is designed for grades 1–8 — ages roughly 6 to 14. It covers addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division, with adaptive difficulty that meets students where they are. That includes early first-graders working on basic addition and older students needing fact fluency remediation before algebra — a range most other fact apps skip.
How is this different from XtraMath? +
XtraMath is a beloved nonprofit that helped popularize daily fact practice — and it's nearly free. But its own reviews on Common Sense Media consistently describe it as boring, stressful, and repetitive, with kids fighting parents about opening it. That's the gap Math Fact Expedition fills. Same evidence-based fluency approach, but with an ocean expedition kids actually want to log into, adaptive pacing instead of a punishing timer, and richer progress reports for parents and MTSS. A tool your child refuses to use is worth $0, no matter the price.
How is this different from Reflex or Prodigy? +
Reflex is excellent but built for school district budgets — typically $35–$65 per student per year, paid by the school. Prodigy is heavily gamified but often criticized for prioritizing game mechanics over math practice, and its business model relies on selling parents an upgrade. Math Fact Expedition keeps the math central, the game supportive, and the price accessible to individual teachers ($49/yr) and families ($29/yr) without district approval.
What does "lifetime" actually mean? +
Founding Members get access for the full life of the product, with a 5-year minimum guarantee. If Math Fact Expedition shuts down before 5 years (it won't), you get a full refund. Practically: you pay $59 once and never pay again, and your access transfers if you change email or move schools.
Is student data private? +
Yes. Student practice data stays local to the device by default. Progress reports are generated for the teacher or parent on that account only — nothing is sold, shared, or used to train AI. COPPA and FERPA-compliant by design, so schools can use it without a lengthy vendor review.
When does the app launch? Am I pre-ordering? +
Yes — Founding Members are pre-ordering. The app is in private beta right now, with a public launch planned for early September 2026, in time for the school year. When you claim Founding Access, you're locking in lifetime pricing and helping shape the product before it opens up. If we don't launch by December 2026, you get a full refund, no questions asked.
Do you offer school or district licenses? +
Yes — school and district pricing is available separately. Get in touch via the email capture below and we'll send a district quote and integration details (Clever, ClassLink, Google Classroom rostering all supported).
